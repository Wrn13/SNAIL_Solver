SNAIL Term Spectrum

Every term of the linear-frame Hamiltonian and of its Schrieffer–Wolff expansion, placed at its detuning δ in the linear frame (it rotates as e−iδt). A term is one normal-ordered monomial with its own coefficient: â₁, â₂ are the two modes, ŝ the SNAIL, and ηk (η*k) marks k net pump photons absorbed (emitted), whose amplitude is already in the coefficient. Order n is everything generated at the n-th step, where each Kerr or g₃X̂³ term counts once. Monomials with the same pump number and net quanta per mode share a detuning and form one signature; a signature stays in the effective Hamiltonian if |δ| is inside the shaded band or if it is resonant (its strength > |δ|), and is otherwise removed by the next step as a whole.
