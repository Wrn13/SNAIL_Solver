SNAIL Term Spectrum

Every term of the linear-frame Hamiltonian and of its Schrieffer–Wolff expansion, placed at its detuning δ in the linear frame (it rotates as e−iδt). Operators are â₁, â₂ for the two modes and ŝ for the SNAIL, with η (η*) for each pump factor. Order n is everything generated at the n-th step, where each Kerr or g₃X̂³ term counts once. A term stays in the effective Hamiltonian if |δ| is inside the shaded band or if it is resonant (strength > |δ|); every other term is removed by the next step.
